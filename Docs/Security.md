# Security

## Security Architecture of NoxGuard

---

# Overview

Security is fundamental to NoxGuard.

Rather than implementing its own confidential computing infrastructure, NoxGuard leverages **iExec Nox** as the trusted execution layer for Confidential Business Policies and Confidential Business Lifecycle Management.

This separation allows NoxGuard to focus on business policy orchestration while relying on Nox for confidential computation, Trusted Execution Environments (TEE), cryptographic attestation, and verifiable execution.

---

# Security Philosophy

Traditional smart contracts secure transactions.

NoxGuard extends this model by securing the **institutional intelligence** that governs those transactions.

Institutional security requires protecting:

- Business Policies
- Rights Evaluation
- Risk Models
- Lending Policies
- Portfolio Strategies
- Compliance Rules
- Lifecycle Decisions
- Proprietary Operational Logic

NoxGuard enables these policies to remain confidential throughout execution.

# Separation of Responsibilities

## NoxGuard

Responsible for:

- Business Policy Organization
- Lifecycle Management
- Workflow Orchestration
- Rights Evaluation
- Policy Verification

NoxGuard does **not** execute confidential computation.

---

## iExec Nox

Responsible for:

- Confidential Computation
- Trusted Execution Environments (TEE)
- Intel TDX-based Secure Execution
- Cryptographic Attestation
- Verifiable Execution

Nox determines **how confidential execution occurs**.

---

## Ethereum

Responsible for:

- Consensus
- Settlement
- Final State Verification
- Transaction Ordering

Only the final verified state transition is recorded on-chain.

# Trusted Execution

NoxGuard relies on iExec Nox to perform confidential computation inside Trusted Execution Environments.

Trusted execution provides:

- Confidential computation
- Memory isolation
- Runtime integrity
- Secure execution
- Remote attestation
- Verifiable execution

NoxGuard does not replace these capabilities.

It consumes them.

---

# Cryptographic Attestation

Every confidential policy evaluation can be accompanied by cryptographic evidence generated by iExec Nox.

Attestation enables participants to verify:

- The expected code executed.
- Execution occurred inside a trusted environment.
- Confidential computation completed successfully.

This provides confidence without revealing confidential business information.

---

# Protection Against Information Exposure

Traditional smart contracts expose:

- Business Logic
- Approval Policies
- Workflow Decisions
- Operational Intelligence

NoxGuard minimizes unnecessary exposure by ensuring confidential policy evaluations occur through iExec Nox before only the verified outcome is committed to Ethereum.

Transparency remains available where blockchain verification is required.

Confidentiality is preserved where institutional business intelligence must remain private.

# Security Principles

NoxGuard follows six security principles.

## Confidential by Design

Business policy evaluation remains confidential.

---

## Separation of Responsibilities

NoxGuard organizes confidential workflows.

Nox executes confidential computation.

Ethereum settles transactions.

---

## Least Information Exposure

Only the minimum information required for settlement is committed on-chain.

---

## Verifiable Confidentiality

Confidential execution is supported by cryptographic attestation through iExec Nox.

---

## Existing Protocol Compatibility

Existing Solidity applications remain unchanged.

---

## Institutional Trust

Business policies remain confidential without sacrificing blockchain verifiability.

# Conclusion

NoxGuard extends existing Solidity smart contracts with Confidential Business Lifecycle Management while relying entirely on **iExec Nox** for confidential computation.

By separating business policy orchestration from confidential execution, NoxGuard enables institutional applications to protect proprietary operational intelligence without compromising blockchain transparency, composability, or verifiability.

Security in NoxGuard is not achieved by replacing blockchain transparency.

It is achieved by ensuring that only the **necessary outcome** becomes public while the **institutional intelligence governing that outcome remains confidential**.
